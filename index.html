Julián Medrano Hoyos | Interdisciplinary Reasearcher

I’m an interdisciplinary researcher exploring culture, society, and technology. My work spans cultural strategy, academic research, and blockchain and AI systems—from decentralized infrastructures to protocol design, on-chain analysis, and algorithmic tools.

With a background in art and philosophy, I combine hands-on technical work with critical inquiry, producing essays, reports and analyses that explore how emerging technologies are shaping the world around us. I’m currently expanding into independent writing and consulting, while continuing research in blockchain and AI applications. Here you can explore my detailed curriculum and my projects.

Alongside this, I collaborate with Fundación Archosauria, supporting long-term strategy and development for a nature reserve in Colombia.
